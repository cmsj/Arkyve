Creating folders

There are several ways to create new folders in an archive:

1. Right click on a file/folder in an archive and choose "New Archive"

2. Select "New Folder" from the Items menu

 

After choosing either of those options, a new folder will be created in the archive with the name "Untitled Folder" and it will be immediately selected for renaming.

Enter the name you want for the folder

 

Important Note:

The archive is not immediately saved to disk, you must manually save your changes or they will be lost
