Using Arkyve from Finder

Archive offers several integrations with macOS Finder, including two Quick Actions extensions and a Quick Look extension.

To enable these, please open System Settings and search for "Extensions", you can then browse the different types of extension and look for "Quick Look" and "Finder" to enable the extensions provided by Arkyve.

 

Quick Look

This extension allows you to click on any supported archive in Finder and press the Space button to show a preview of the contents of the archive.

 

Quick Actions

Arkyve provides two action extensions for Finder, which you can use via the "Quick Actions" menu that appears when you right click on a file":

  • Extract
  • Compress

The Extract action will extract the entire contents of an archive into the current folder.

The Compress action will take the one/several files or folders you have selected, and compress them into a new archive, with a small UI for you to select the name of the archive and which of the supported formats it should use.
